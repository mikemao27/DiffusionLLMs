<h1 align = "center">Reading List: Toward Substantial Wall-Clock Speedups in Diffusion LLMs</h1>

Here, we provide a working reading list on techniques that can yield runtime wall-clock speedups in both auto-regressive and diffusion large-language models. These techniques are not specific to any particular type of model, and can be generally used across the board. We utilize some of these techniques ourselves in the development of the KINETIC kernel.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> These papers are all available in public domain. However, note that perhaps not all of these papers (but a majority of which) are provided here in PDF form. We do not claim to own any of the ideas or intellectual property behind these papers, but instead merely provide them as resources to gain an insight into the ideas that shaped the models we produce. To assist with paper retrieval, we provide links to each paper.

## Papers

* [Kwon et al. - "Efficient Memory Management for Large Language Model Serving with PagedAttention" (2023)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2309.06180)
* [Nie et al. - "Large Language Diffusion Models" (2025)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2502.09992)
* [Sahoo et al. - "Simple and Effective Masked Diffusion Language Models" (2024)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2406.07524)
* [Wu et al. - "Fast-dLLM: Training-free Acceleration of Diffusion LLM by Enabling KV Cache and Parallel Decoding" (2025)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2505.22618)
* [Zhao et al. - "SkipGPT: Dynamic Layer Pruning Reinvented with Token Awareness and Module Decoupling" (2025)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2506.04179)
* [Zhu et al. - "ES-dLLM: Efficient Inference for Diffusion Large Language Models by Early-Skipping" (2026)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.10088)
